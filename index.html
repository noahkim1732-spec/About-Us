About Us

Making college and career planning easier to understand, easier to access, and less overwhelming for every student.

Our Mission

Helping students move forward with confidence

Focusing on the Future is a website built to help students navigate through the challenging and confusing college application process. The website has plenty of organized resources to use and an AI bot to help with making decisions.

?

The Problem

The information related to college is often available from different sources, and it is neither easy to comprehend nor accessible when needed. Freshmen in colleges can feel confused about getting financial aid, college requirements, and career options without getting any professional guidance.

→

What We Do

The credible information is organized into brief guides, we link users to other useful websites and also include student perspectives in order to make college more real to them. The goal is to help users find the next piece of helpful information.
